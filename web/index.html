Frequently asked questions

Is T2G CRM suitable for small businesses in Tamil Nadu?

Absolutely. T2G CRM is built specifically for Tamil Nadu and Indian SMBs — manufacturers, wholesalers, service providers, retailers, jewellers, real estate and agencies. It supports IndiaMART, JustDial, TradeIndia and WhatsApp leads, GST invoicing, and comes with local support.

Does it support GST billing and invoices?

Yes. Create GST-compliant quotations and invoices with HSN/SAC codes, multiple tax slabs, inter-state and intra-state GST, payment tracking and professional templates — in seconds.

Can it capture leads from IndiaMART, JustDial and WhatsApp?

Yes. T2G CRM connects directly to IndiaMART, JustDial, TradeIndia and Google Sheets, and sends automatic WhatsApp notifications to your leads and customers, so no enquiry is ever missed.

How much does it cost?

T2G CRM is free to start, with affordable plans that scale as your team grows. No expensive per-user enterprise fees like global CRMs.

Do I need technical knowledge to use it?

Not at all. It's designed to be simple. You can set up your account, add your team and start capturing leads the same day — no consultant required.

Is my business data safe and private?

Yes. Each business gets its own isolated, secure workspace with role-based permissions, so your team only sees what they should and your data stays completely private.
